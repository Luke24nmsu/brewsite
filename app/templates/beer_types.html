My Favorite: Hazy IPA

My personal favorite beer style is a Hazy IPA. I like its hazy appearance, juicy citrus and tropical fruit flavors, smooth feel, and usually softer bitterness. To me, the vibe is a laid-back afternoon around a mountain campfire with good friends and no rush, maybe a fishing pole in hand. My go-to IPA is 7K from Santa Fe Brewing Company.

12 Common Beer Types

  1. Lager: Many familiar pale lagers are crisp, clean, and refreshing.
  2. Pilsner: A pale lager with a crisp finish and noticeable hop flavor.
  3. Blonde Ale: Light and approachable with gentle malt sweetness.
  4. Pale Ale: A balance of malt flavor and citrusy or floral hops.
  5. IPA: India Pale Ale, known for bold hop aromas and flavors.
  6. Hazy IPA: Juicy and fruit-forward with a smooth feel and softer bitterness.
  7. Amber Ale: Amber-colored with caramel and toasted malt flavors.
  8. Brown Ale: Malty with nutty, caramel, or light chocolate flavors.
  9. Wheat Beer: Brewed with wheat; flavors vary from citrus to banana and clove.
  10. Porter: A dark beer with chocolate and toasted malt flavors.
  11. Stout: A dark beer often featuring roasted coffee or chocolate flavors.
  12. Sour Beer: Tart and tangy, sometimes brewed with fruit.
